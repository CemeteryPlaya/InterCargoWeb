Order cancellation (withdrawal from the service)

  • Until the parcel arrives at our warehouse in China (status “Added by user”), you can withdraw from the service at any time — just delete the tracking number in your personal account. No charge applies.
  • After the parcel arrives at the warehouse in China and before dispatch, you may withdraw by request to customer support; the parcel is returned to the seller or handed over to a person you designate in China, at the customer's expense.
  • Once the parcel has been dispatched from the warehouse in China, cancellation is not possible: the cargo is in transit and cleared as part of a consolidated shipment.

Defective or incomplete goods

{{ COMPANY.brand }} is not the seller of the goods. Claims about quality, completeness, size and warranty are made to the marketplace seller under that platform's rules (most platforms accept claims within a few days of receipt). Our managers will help with the correspondence and provide photos and the weight of the parcel from our warehouse.

If damage or shortage occurred through our fault (the packaging was intact on acceptance at the warehouse in China and was damaged in transit):

  1. Inspect the parcel at the pickup point on collection. If the packaging is visibly damaged, ask the pickup point staff to record it (photo, note).
  2. Within 3 days of collection, contact customer support with the receipt number, tracking number, photos of the parcel and the item, and a screenshot of the order with the price.
  3. We will review the claim within 10 calendar days and, at the customer's choice, refund the delivery cost of the damaged part of the parcel and the confirmed value of the damaged or lost item, or arrange delivery of a replacement if the seller sends one.

We are not liable for damage caused by the seller's inadequate packaging if the customer did not order crating, or for latent defects of the goods.
