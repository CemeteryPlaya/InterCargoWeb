2. Data we process

  • account data: login, password (stored only as an irreversible hash), first name, last name;
  • contact data: phone number, e-mail address, delivery address (for door-to-door delivery), selected pickup point;
  • order data: tracking numbers, parcel weights and statuses, receipts, handover history;

3. Purposes of processing

  • registering and identifying the user, concluding and performing the agreement (public offer);
  • receiving, delivering and handing over parcels, creating receipts, accepting payments and making refunds;
  • parcel status notifications and replies to enquiries;
  • fraud prevention, account protection, handling disputed payments (chargebacks);
  • compliance with the laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan (including tax and customs law);
  • improving the Website (anonymised visit statistics).

4. Legal grounds

Processing is based on the data subject's consent given at registration (Consent to personal data processing), as well as on the need to perform the agreement and comply with the law.

5. Disclosure to third parties

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only to the extent necessary to provide the services:

  • Halyk Bank of Kazakhstan JSC — to process payments and refunds (Halyk ePay service);
  • transport and customs clearance partners — recipient data required to clear the cargo;
  • notification services (e-mail, Telegram, browser push services) — only the contact and the notification text;
  • state authorities — on grounds provided by law.

Recipients must keep the data confidential and secure.

6. Storage and protection

  • Personal data is stored on servers of the hosting provider PS.kz in Almaty (Republic of Kazakhstan).
  • Only authorised employees have access to the data, to the extent needed for their duties; access rights are role-based.
  • Data exchanged between the browser and the Website is protected by HTTPS (TLS); passwords are stored as hashes.
  • Data is kept for as long as the account exists and, after its deletion, for the periods required by law (including for accounting and payment documents), after which it is destroyed.

7. Cookies

The Website uses cookies required for signing in, form protection (CSRF), remembering the language, and anonymised visit statistics. Disabling cookies in your browser may make signing in impossible.
