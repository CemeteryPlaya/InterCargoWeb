3. Subject of the Agreement

3.1. 4. Service procedure

4.1. The Customer registers on the Website, provides accurate details and selects a pickup point.

4.2. The Customer places a purchase with the seller, specifying the warehouse address in China and their identifier, and adds the tracking number to the personal account. Parcels without a tracking number in the personal account may not be identified.

4.3. The Contractor receives and weighs the parcel and ships it to Kazakhstan as part of a consolidated shipment. The average delivery time is 8–12 days from dispatch from the warehouse in China; delivery times are estimates.

4.4. When the parcels arrive in Kazakhstan, a Receipt is created. The parcel is handed over at the pickup point upon presentation of the paid Receipt's QR code, or passed to a courier for door-to-door delivery.
