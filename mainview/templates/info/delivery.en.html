Delivery times

  • From China to the sorting warehouse in Kazakhstan — 8–12 days on average from dispatch from our warehouse in China.
  • From the sorting warehouse to the pickup point — usually 1–3 days.
  • Delivery may take longer during holidays (including in China), sales seasons and customs congestion. The seller's delivery time to our warehouse in China is not included.

The current status of every parcel is shown in your personal account; you are notified when the status changes.

Delivery methods

  • Pickup point. The pickup point is chosen at registration and can be changed in profile settings. Show the QR code of the paid receipt to collect your parcel.
  • Door-to-door delivery — where available; ask a manager for terms and price.

The parcel is stored at the pickup point until collected. If it is not collected within 10 days, we will remind you in your personal account and in Telegram.

Restrictions

The following are not accepted for shipping:

  • weapons (including cold steel, air guns and replicas), ammunition, explosive, flammable, radioactive, toxic, corrosive and infectious substances;
  • narcotic and psychotropic substances, precursors, medicines that require an import permit;
  • alcohol and tobacco products, e-cigarettes and e-liquids;
  • perishable food and liquids (except products in factory vacuum packaging);
  • live animals, plants, seeds;
  • drones and other goods whose import into the Republic of Kazakhstan is prohibited or restricted by EAEU and Kazakhstan law;
  • goods infringing intellectual property rights and other goods prohibited from circulation.

Electronics (phones, laptops) and fragile items are accepted at the customer's risk; for fragile items ask the seller for reinforced packaging or order wooden crating.

A parcel containing prohibited items may be detained by customs; in this case the customer bears the costs and responsibility.

Export and licensing restrictions

Goods for personal use are imported within the limits set by the EAEU Customs Code and decisions of the Eurasian Economic Commission. Goods that require licences, permits or certificates for import (including restrictions of the exporting country) are not accepted unless the customer provides the relevant documents. Commercial consignments are cleared by agreement with a manager.
