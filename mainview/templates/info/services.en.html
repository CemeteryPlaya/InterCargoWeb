{{ COMPANY.brand }} provides parcel delivery services to the Republic of Kazakhstan. We do not sell marketplace goods: you buy the goods from the seller of your choice, and we receive them at our warehouse, transport them, handle customs clearance and hand them over at a pickup point or by courier.

1. Parcel delivery from China to Kazakhstan

What the service includes

  • receiving the parcel at our warehouse in China (the warehouse address is shown in your personal account after registration);
  • matching the tracking number to your account, weighing;
  • consolidation and transportation of the cargo to Kazakhstan;
  • customs clearance of the cargo;
  • sorting and delivery to the selected pickup point or to your door;
  • status tracking in your personal account and notifications (website, push, Telegram);
  • storage at the pickup point and handover by the receipt QR code.

Characteristics and limitations

  • order weight — from 0 to 100 kg (over 100 kg — by agreement with a manager);
  • delivery time — 8–12 days on average from dispatch from our warehouse in China;
  • prohibited goods are not accepted — see “Delivery”;
  • guarantee: we are responsible for the safety of the parcel from its arrival at our warehouse in China until it is handed over to you (terms — public offer, section 7). The warranty for the goods themselves, their completeness and quality is the seller's obligation.

3. How to place an order — step by step

  1. Registration. Fill in the form on the home page: login, phone, pickup point, e-mail; accept the public offer and give consent to personal data processing.
  2. Warehouse address. Copy our warehouse address in China from your personal account and enter it when ordering on a marketplace (Pinduoduo, Taobao, Poizon, 1688, etc.).
  3. Tracking number. After the seller ships, add the parcel's tracking number to your personal account so we know whose parcel it is.
  4. Tracking. Follow the statuses: “Arrived at warehouse (China)” → “Shipped from warehouse (China)” → “Arrived at customs” → “Arrived in Almaty” → “Delivered to sorting warehouse” → “Out for delivery” → “Delivered to pickup point”.
  5. Receipt. When the parcel arrives in Kazakhstan, a receipt with the weight and the amount due is created.
  6. Payment. Pay the receipt online by bank card in “Receipts” or at the pickup point — see “Payment”.
  7. Collection. Show the receipt QR code at the pickup point and collect your parcel, or receive it from the courier.

Video and interactive guides are available in “Tutorial”.

Licences and permits

Freight forwarding (delivery arrangement) services are not a licensed activity in the Republic of Kazakhstan; no special licences or permits are required to provide them. Customs clearance is carried out in accordance with the legislation of the EAEU and the Republic of Kazakhstan.
